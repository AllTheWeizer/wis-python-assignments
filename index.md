# Joseph Borenstein

**Neuroscience | Computational biology | Weizmann Institute of Science**

Welcome! I study how neural circuits process information and respond to changes in brain state. My work combines experimental neuroscience with computational analysis.

## Research interests

- **Prefrontal cortical circuits:** Understanding how inhibition shapes population activity and responses to sensory or behavioral events.
- **Chronic stress:** Studying changes in medial prefrontal cortical function, including somatostatin-expressing (SST) interneurons.
- **Neural recording and analysis:** Neuropixels electrophysiology, optogenetics, spike sorting, and Python-based data analysis.
- **Computational biology:** Developing reproducible analysis tools for biological data.

## Ph.D. research project

**Predictive Prefrontal Microcircuit Models of Chronic Stress and Anhedonia**

My proposed doctoral research investigates how chronic stress alters prefrontal network activity and the function of somatostatin-expressing (SST) interneurons in mice. It combines high-density Neuropixels recordings in the prelimbic and infralimbic cortices, SST-targeted optogenetics, computational circuit models, and repeated measurements of reward-directed behavior to examine how stress-related neural changes relate to anhedonia and whether targeted stimulation can shift network activity toward non-stressed patterns.
